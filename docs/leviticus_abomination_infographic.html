"unto the LORD"
Leviticus never uses the phrase "abomination unto the LORD" (that pairing belongs to Deuteronomy). Here the word is either aimed at the people directly ("unto you"), framed as something they must actively regard as detestable, stated flatly with no addressee, or bundled up afterward as "these abominations."
