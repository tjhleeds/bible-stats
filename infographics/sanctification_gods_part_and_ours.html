God’s Part and Ours

“Sanctify yourselves” and “I am the Lord which sanctify you” run side by side through the Old Testament. Here is every verse of each.

Leviticus 20:7

Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy: for I am the Lord your God.

⇄
Leviticus 20:8

And ye shall keep my statutes, and do them: I am the Lord which sanctify you.

Two consecutive verses state both sides: the people are to sanctify themselves, and it is the Lord who sanctifies them.

God’s refrain is concentrated in Leviticus7 of the 10 “I the Lord … sanctify” verses are in Leviticus, all of them in chapters 20–22. Ezekiel repeats it twice, both times as something Israel or the nations will come to know.
“Against to morrow”Several commands to sanctify come the day before God acts: Sinai (Exodus 19:10–11), the quails (Numbers 11:18), the Jordan (Joshua 3:5), Achan (Joshua 7:13).
The Levites outdo the priestsIn Hezekiah’s reform the priests were “too few”, and the Passover itself was delayed because they “had not sanctified themselves sufficiently” (2 Chronicles 29:34, 30:3).
“Be ye holy; for I am holy”The reason given is always God’s own holiness. The phrase “am holy” is in 7 verses: five in Leviticus, one in Psalms, and Peter quoting it in 1 Peter 1:16.

The queries

“Sanctify yourselves” — the 24 verses on the left

SELECT b.name AS book, v.chapter, v.verse, v.text
FROM KJV_verses v
JOIN KJV_books b ON b.id = v.book_id
WHERE v.text LIKE '%sanctify yourselves%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%sanctify now yourselves%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%sanctify themselves%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%sanctified themselves%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%sanctify myself%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%consecrate yourselves%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%consecrated yourselves%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%consecrate themselves%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%consecrate himself%'
ORDER BY v.book_id, v.chapter, v.verse

“I the Lord … sanctify” — the 10 refrain verses

FROM KJV_verses v
JOIN KJV_books b ON b.id = v.book_id
WHERE v.text LIKE '%Lord that doth sanctify%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%Lord which sanctify%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%Lord, which sanctify%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%Lord do sanctify%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%Lord that sanctify%'
   OR v.text LIKE '%Lord which hallow%'
ORDER BY v.book_id, v.chapter, v.verse
