Who Sanctifies?

In the New Testament, who or what does the sanctifying? The Father, Christ’s offering, the Spirit, the word, faith, and a few surprises.

Christ’s offering is named most11 verses connect sanctification to Christ, five of them in Hebrews: “by one offering he hath perfected for ever them that are sanctified” (10:14).
Father, Son and Spirit together1 Peter 1:2 names all three in one verse, in order: the Father’s foreknowledge, the Spirit’s sanctification, the blood of Jesus Christ. 1 Corinthians 6:11 names the Lord Jesus and “the Spirit of our God” together.
Jesus himself is sanctified“Whom the Father hath sanctified, and sent into the world” (John 10:36); “for their sakes I sanctify myself” (John 17:19).
Sanctification can be sharedIn 1 Corinthians 7:14 holiness spreads from a believing spouse to an unbelieving one and to the children: “else were your children unclean; but now are they holy”.

Things that sanctify other things

5 verses
Five verses where the sanctifier is not God or a means of grace
  • Matthew 23:17
    Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifieth the gold?The temple sanctifies the gold.
  • Matthew 23:19
    Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifieth the gift?The altar sanctifies the gift.
  • 1 Corinthians 7:14
    For the unbelieving husband is sanctified by the wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified by the husband: else were your children unclean; but now are they holy.A believing spouse sanctifies an unbelieving one.
  • 1 Timothy 4:5
    For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.Food is sanctified by the word of God and prayer (4:3–4).
  • Hebrews 9:13
    For if the blood of bulls and of goats, and the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean, sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh:The blood of bulls and goats sanctified “to the purifying of the flesh” — the comparison that 9:14 completes with “how much more … the blood of Christ”.

The query

The 46 New Testament verses

SELECT b.name AS book, v.chapter, v.verse, v.text
FROM KJV_verses v
JOIN KJV_books b ON b.id = v.book_id
WHERE v.book_id >= 40
  AND (v.text LIKE '%sanctif%' OR v.text LIKE '%hallow%' OR v.text LIKE '%consecrat%'
       OR v.text LIKE '%holiness%' OR v.text LIKE '%holily%')
ORDER BY v.book_id, v.chapter, v.verse

Run this query in Datasette Lite, loaded directly from data/KJV.db in this repository. 21 of these name a source and are grouped above; four more appear only in the “things that sanctify” card (1 Timothy 4:5 is in both). The rest (such as “Hallowed be thy name”) are about something other than who sanctifies, and appear on page 5. The grouping itself is editorial and not in the query.
