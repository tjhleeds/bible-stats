The two books use the word quite differently. Proverbs never says "Blessed be the Lord" — not once in its 14 verses; blessing there is almost always something seen in a life, in children, a wife, a reputation, a memory. Psalms splits nearly evenly between pronouncing someone blessed and blessing God in return. Between them the two books hold 67 of the 307 such verses in the whole KJV.
Every verse below was then read with its five neighbouring verses on each side. That moved two verses between themes: Psalm 84:5 (a pilgrimage blessing, not a general one) and Psalm 118:26 (spoken over an arriving worshipper, not over God). It also showed that a search for "blessed" misses the same blessing wherever the King James translators wrote "happy" instead — 10 further verses, including Psalm 128:2, which sits between two of the verses shown here. Where the surrounding verses change or sharpen a reading, the card says so under the quotation.

1 · Trust and refuge in the LORD

6 verses

"Blessed is the man that trusteth in him" — blessing attached not to circumstance but to reliance

  • Psalm 2:12
    Kiss the Son, lest he be angry, and ye perish from the way, when his wrath is kindled but a little. Blessed are all they that put their trust in him.All who take refuge in the Son Addressed to hostile kings and judges (2:10–11): the blessing is the alternative offered to rulers who have just been told to submit.
  • Psalm 34:8
    O taste and see that the Lord is good: blessed is the man that trusteth in him.The one who tastes and trusts The invitation follows a concrete rescue — "This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him" (34:6).
  • Psalm 40:4
    Blessed is that man that maketh the Lord his trust, and respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies.Trust in the LORD over pride and lies Trust here is the response to rescue, not a starting position: 40:1–3 is the pit, the rock and the new song.
  • Psalm 84:12
    O Lord of hosts, blessed is the man that trusteth in thee.Simple trust, stated as a closing refrain Closes Psalm 84, where 84:11 has just promised that no good thing is withheld from them that walk uprightly.
  • Psalm 146:5
    Happy is he that hath the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the Lord his God:Hope set on the God of Jacob Sharpened by the warning it answers: "Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help" (146:3–4).
  • Proverbs 16:20
    He that handleth a matter wisely shall find good: and whoso trusteth in the Lord, happy is he.Trusting the LORD rather than one’s own handling Placed just after "Pride goeth before destruction" (16:18), so the trust is set against self-reliance.

2 · Sin forgiven, iniquity not imputed

2 verses

the only pair in either book where blessing is defined by what is taken away

  • Psalm 32:1
    Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered.Transgression forgiven, sin covered 32:3–5 supplies the route: silence wasted him away until he confessed, "and thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin."
  • Psalm 32:2
    Blessed is the man unto whom the Lord imputeth not iniquity, and in whose spirit there is no guile.Iniquity not reckoned; no guile within "No guile" is defined by the confession of 32:5, not by innocence — the man had sin to hide and stopped hiding it.

3 · The fear of the LORD and a blameless walk

9 verses

"Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD; that walketh in his ways" — conduct as the test

  • Psalm 1:1
    Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful.Refusing the counsel, way and seat of the wicked Only half the blessing is stated in the verse itself; 1:2–3 completes it with delight in the law and the tree planted by the rivers of water.
  • Psalm 106:3
    Blessed are they that keep judgment, and he that doeth righteousness at all times.Keeping judgment, doing righteousness at all times Sharpened by what follows: 106:6 is "We have sinned with our fathers," and the rest of the psalm recounts Israel failing exactly this test.
  • Psalm 112:1
    Praise ye the Lord. Blessed is the man that feareth the Lord, that delighteth greatly in his commandments.Fearing the LORD, delighting in his commandments Runs straight on from 111:10, "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom" — Psalms 111 and 112 are a matched acrostic pair.
  • Psalm 119:1
    Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.Undefiled in the way 119:3–4 glosses "undefiled": they do no iniquity, and God has commanded his precepts to be kept diligently.
  • Psalm 128:1
    Blessed is every one that feareth the Lord; that walketh in his ways.Fearing the LORD and walking in his ways
  • Psalm 128:2
    For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee.Eating the labour of his own hands The middle of a four-verse unit: 128:1 and 128:4 frame it, and 128:3 fills it out with a fruitful wife and children round the table.
  • Psalm 128:4
    Behold, that thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord.The blessing summed up and pointed back at "Thus" points back to 128:2–3: the labour of his hands, a wife as a fruitful vine, children like olive plants.
  • Proverbs 28:14
    Happy is the man that feareth alway: but he that hardeneth his heart shall fall into mischief.The man who fears always Its opposite is given in the same verse — hardening the heart — and 28:13 has just contrasted covering sins with confessing them.
  • Proverbs 29:18
    Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.Keeping the law where there is no vision Sits among sayings on correction (29:15, 29:17): the law does for a people what reproof does for a child.

4 · Hearing wisdom, keeping the word, taking correction

6 verses

"Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates" — attentiveness, including to chastening

  • Psalm 94:12
    Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Lord, and teachest him out of thy law;The man God chastens and teaches 94:13 names the purpose: "that thou mayest give him rest from the days of adversity." The chastening is not the blessing; the rest it buys is.
  • Psalm 119:2
    Blessed are they that keep his testimonies, and that seek him with the whole heart.Keeping his testimonies, seeking him with the whole heart
  • Proverbs 3:13
    Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding.Finding wisdom and getting understanding Follows straight on from "whom the Lord loveth he correcteth" (3:11–12), which puts the finding of wisdom on the far side of correction.
  • Proverbs 3:18
    She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: and happy is every one that retaineth her.Holding wisdom fast once found The tree of life image caps the valuation of 3:14–17: better than silver, more precious than rubies, length of days in her right hand.
  • Proverbs 8:32
    Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children: for blessed are they that keep my ways.Keeping the ways of Wisdom The speaker is Wisdom herself, who has just described standing beside God at the creation of the world (8:22–31).
  • Proverbs 8:34
    Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.Daily, watchful hearing The gates and doors are Wisdom’s own house — the house she builds, with seven pillars, in 9:1.

5 · Mercy to the poor

4 verses

the one theme both books state in almost the same terms

  • Psalm 41:1
    Blessed is he that considereth the poor: the Lord will deliver him in time of trouble.Considering the poor Claimed from weakness, not strength: 41:4–9 shows the psalmist himself sick, slandered and betrayed by his own familiar friend.
  • Psalm 41:2
    The Lord will preserve him, and keep him alive; and he shall be blessed upon the earth: and thou wilt not deliver him unto the will of his enemies.The promised outcome for that man "He" is still the man of 41:1 who considered the poor; the promise runs straight on.
  • Proverbs 14:21
    He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he.Mercy on the poor rather than contempt Answers 14:20 directly: "The poor is hated even of his own neighbour: but the rich hath many friends."
  • Proverbs 22:9
    He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor.The bountiful eye that shares bread 22:7 has just described the rich ruling over the poor and the borrower serving the lender; the bountiful eye reverses that flow.

6 · Brought near: chosen, gathered, welcomed in

8 verses

blessing tied to the house of God and to God’s own choosing rather than to the person chosen

  • Psalm 33:12
    Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord; and the people whom he hath chosen for his own inheritance.The nation whose God is the LORD Set directly against 33:10, where the Lord brings the counsel of the heathen to nought — the contrast is national, not individual.
  • Psalm 65:4
    Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, even of thy holy temple.The man God chooses and brings into his courts 65:3 is what makes the approach possible: "as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away."
  • Psalm 84:4
    Blessed are they that dwell in thy house: they will be still praising thee. Selah.Those who dwell in God’s house The house is the temple longed for in 84:1–3, where even the sparrow has found a nest by the altars.
  • Psalm 84:5
    Blessed is the man whose strength is in thee; in whose heart are the ways of them.The pilgrim on the road to Zion 84:6–7 shows the "ways" in his heart are pilgrim roads: through the valley of Baca, "from strength to strength," until "in Zion appeareth before God." A pilgrimage blessing, not a general remark about inner strength.
  • Psalm 89:15
    Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance.The people who know the joyful sound The sound is festal acclamation of the throne described in 89:14, founded on justice, judgment, mercy and truth.
  • Psalm 115:15
    Ye are blessed of the Lord which made heaven and earth.Israel addressed directly as blessed A declaration over the whole congregation: 115:13 has just blessed "them that fear the Lord, both small and great."
  • Psalm 118:26
    Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord: we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.Benediction on the worshipper arriving at the temple Not praise directed at God: the second half of the verse names the speakers and their target — "we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord" — and 118:19–20 has just opened the gates of righteousness to let him in.
  • Psalm 144:15
    Happy is that people, that is in such a case: yea, happy is that people, whose God is the Lord.The people whose God is the LORD The second half re-reads the first: 144:12–14 has listed full barns, strong oxen and no complaining in the streets, then the verse locates the real cause elsewhere.

7 · Blessing that lands on a life: seed, household, name

12 verses

blessing as something observed — in children, harvest, reputation and memory

  • Psalm 21:6
    For thou hast made him most blessed for ever: thou hast made him exceeding glad with thy countenance.The king, made most blessed for ever A royal psalm: 21:1–5 itemises what the king was given — his heart’s desire, a crown of pure gold, length of days for ever.
  • Psalm 37:22
    For such as be blessed of him shall inherit the earth; and they that be cursed of him shall be cut off.Those blessed of him inherit the earth "Of him" is the Lord. The verse is one half of a blessed-and-cursed antithesis that runs the length of Psalm 37.
  • Psalm 37:26
    He is ever merciful, and lendeth; and his seed is blessed.The merciful lender’s seed Grounded in observation rather than promise: 37:25 is "I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken."
  • Psalm 45:2
    Thou art fairer than the children of men: grace is poured into thy lips: therefore God hath blessed thee for ever.The king blessed for ever Addressed to the king at his wedding (45:1, "the things which I have made touching the king"), not to a private person.
  • Psalm 72:17
    His name shall endure for ever: his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and men shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed.The king through whom the nations are blessed The king is the channel as well as the recipient — men are blessed in him, echoing the promise to Abraham. 72:20 then closes the prayers of David.
  • Psalm 112:2
    His seed shall be mighty upon earth: the generation of the upright shall be blessed.The generation of the upright "His" is the God-fearing man of 112:1; the seed inherits the standing the father was blessed with.
  • Psalm 127:5
    Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them: they shall not be ashamed, but they shall speak with the enemies in the gate.The man with a quiver full of children Conditioned by the two verses before it: unless the Lord builds the house the builders labour in vain, and children are "an heritage of the Lord" (127:1–3).
  • Psalm 147:13
    For he hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; he hath blessed thy children within thee.Children within Jerusalem’s gates Addressed to the city (147:12, "Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem"), so "thy children" are its inhabitants, not one family.
  • Proverbs 5:18
    Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth.A man’s wife, under the figure of his own spring The fountain is the wife: 5:15–17 has just told him to drink from his own cistern and keep those waters "only thine own, and not strangers’ with thee."
  • Proverbs 10:7
    The memory of the just is blessed: but the name of the wicked shall rot.The memory of the just Paired with the line immediately before it: "Blessings are upon the head of the just" (10:6).
  • Proverbs 20:7
    The just man walketh in his integrity: his children are blessed after him.The just man’s children after him Answers the question asked one verse earlier: "a faithful man who can find?" (20:6).
  • Proverbs 31:28
    Her children arise up, and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praiseth her.The virtuous woman, called blessed at home 31:30 gives the ground of it: "a woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praised" — which ties this verse back to theme 3.

8 · "Blessed be the LORD" — blessing returned to God

16 verses

the largest group: not a description of anyone’s state, but praise directed back at God

  • Psalm 18:46
    The Lord liveth; and blessed be my rock; and let the God of my salvation be exalted.Blessed be my rock
  • Psalm 28:6
    Blessed be the Lord, because he hath heard the voice of my supplications.For hearing supplication The answer to the plea of 28:2, "Hear the voice of my supplications, when I cry unto thee."
  • Psalm 31:21
    Blessed be the Lord: for he hath shewed me his marvellous kindness in a strong city.For marvellous kindness
  • Psalm 41:13
    Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting, and to everlasting. Amen, and Amen.Doxology closing Book I
  • Psalm 66:20
    Blessed be God, which hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me.For not turning away prayer 66:18 has just conceded the condition: "If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me."
  • Psalm 68:19
    Blessed be the Lord, who daily loadeth us with benefits, even the God of our salvation. Selah.For daily benefits
  • Psalm 68:35
    O God, thou art terrible out of thy holy places: the God of Israel is he that giveth strength and power unto his people. Blessed be God.For strength given to his people
  • Psalm 72:18
    Blessed be the Lord God, the God of Israel, who only doeth wondrous things.Doxology closing Book II
  • Psalm 72:19
    And blessed be his glorious name for ever: and let the whole earth be filled with his glory; Amen, and Amen.Blessed be his glorious name
  • Psalm 89:52
    Blessed be the Lord for evermore. Amen, and Amen.Doxology closing Book III Placed immediately after the psalm’s complaint that God has cast off his anointed (89:49–51) — praise offered without the complaint being resolved.
  • Psalm 106:48
    Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting to everlasting: and let all the people say, Amen. Praise ye the Lord.Doxology closing Book IV
  • Psalm 113:2
    Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth and for evermore.Blessed be the name, for evermore
  • Psalm 119:12
    Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thy statutes.Blessed art thou — praise joined to a request Sits among petitions (119:10–11): the praise is the setting for "teach me thy statutes," not a separate act.
  • Psalm 124:6
    Blessed be the Lord, who hath not given us as a prey to their teeth.For deliverance from the enemy
  • Psalm 135:21
    Blessed be the Lord out of Zion, which dwelleth at Jerusalem. Praise ye the Lord.Blessed be the LORD out of Zion Answers the fourfold call of 135:19–20, "Bless the Lord, O house of Israel... O house of Aaron... O house of Levi."
  • Psalm 144:1
    Blessed be the Lord my strength, which teacheth my hands to war, and my fingers to fight:For strength and training for war

9 · Where blessing fails

2 verses

blessing pronounced on oneself, and blessing that never arrives

  • Psalm 49:18
    Though while he lived he blessed his soul: and men will praise thee, when thou doest well to thyself.The rich man blessing his own soul The man is the rich one of 49:16–17 who "shall carry nothing away" when he dies. He congratulates himself inside a life already accounted for.
  • Proverbs 20:21
    An inheritance may be gotten hastily at the beginning; but the end thereof shall not be blessed.An inheritance gained in haste 20:20 has just put out the lamp of whoever curses father or mother, which may colour how the inheritance was got hold of so early.

10 · Blessing pronounced on vengeance

2 verses

the hardest case in either book — the formula turned on Babylon

  • Psalm 137:8
    O daughter of Babylon, who art to be destroyed; happy shall he be, that rewardeth thee as thou hast served us.Whoever repays Babylon in kind The psalm of exile: 137:1–4 is captivity by the rivers of Babylon, where the captors demanded a song of Zion, and 137:7 has just asked God to remember what Edom did.
  • Psalm 137:9
    Happy shall he be, that taketh and dasheth thy little ones against the stones.Whoever dashes her children on the stones The measure is set by 137:8, "as thou hast served us" — the verse asks for what Babylon did to Jerusalem, not more. Psalm 138:1 then opens with unbroken praise.

The queries

Every verse in play — the 67 verses above

SELECT b.name AS book, v.chapter, v.verse, v.text
FROM KJV_verses v
JOIN KJV_books b ON b.id = v.book_id
WHERE b.name IN ('Psalms', 'Proverbs')
  AND (v.text LIKE '%blessed%' OR v.text LIKE '%happy%')
ORDER BY v.book_id, v.chapter, v.verse

Run this query in Datasette Lite, loaded directly from data/KJV.db in this repository. LIKE is case-insensitive for ASCII in SQLite, so this catches "Blessed" and "blessed", "Happy" and "happy" alike. Both words are needed: the King James translators render the same pronouncement either way, sometimes within a single passage — Psalm 128 alternates between them across four consecutive verses. Grouping the results into the ten themes above is editorial, done by reading them; it is not in the query.

Verses that call someone blessed — 32 of the 33

SELECT b.name AS book, v.chapter, v.verse, v.text
FROM KJV_verses v
JOIN KJV_books b ON b.id = v.book_id
WHERE b.name IN ('Psalms', 'Proverbs')
  AND (v.text LIKE '%lessed is%' OR v.text LIKE '%lessed are%'
       OR v.text LIKE '%appy is%' OR v.text LIKE '%appy shall%')
ORDER BY v.book_id, v.chapter, v.verse

Run this query in Datasette Lite. Matching on lessed and appy without their first letters keeps both the sentence-initial and the mid-sentence spellings without needing four more patterns. The thirty-third is Psalm 118:26, "Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord," which uses the pattern of the next query while blessing a person rather than God.

Verses that bless God — 16 verses

SELECT b.name AS book, v.chapter, v.verse, v.text
FROM KJV_verses v
JOIN KJV_books b ON b.id = v.book_id
WHERE b.name IN ('Psalms', 'Proverbs')
  AND v.text LIKE '%Blessed be%'
ORDER BY v.book_id, v.chapter, v.verse

Run this query in Datasette Lite. It returns 16 rows, but not the same 16. Psalm 118:26 blesses a person, so it is grouped under theme 6; Psalm 119:12 takes its place in theme 8, since "Blessed art thou, O Lord" addresses God directly without matching this pattern.
